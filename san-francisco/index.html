Keeping Your San Francisco HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns start with maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and starting to struggle, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Common HVAC Problems in San Francisco

Most local HVAC failures give warning signs before they happen. A breaker that trips more than once, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're an affordable repair. Left alone, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We diagnose the cause, not just the symptom, so small problems stay small.
