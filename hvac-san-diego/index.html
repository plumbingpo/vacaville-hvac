When Your System Fails in San Diego

Heating and cooling failures rarely wait for a convenient day, which is why we offer prompt, flexible scheduling for most situations. When you call, you'll speak with a real person who gets a technician headed to your door without delay. Our typical arrival window in the area is typically within a day for urgent calls, and a dead AC in a heat wave or a furnace out on a freezing night is exactly the kind of call we move to the front of the line.

Here's what we see most often when something breaks at home: warm air from the AC, a condenser that hums but won't start, a furnace that ignites and shuts off, a breaker that keeps tripping, an evaporator coil that has iced over, a condensate drain overflowing into the furnace cabinet, or a thermostat that has gone blank. Running it frozen can flood the drain pan and damage the compressor. A furnace that keeps short-cycling, or any burning smell, means it's time to kill power at the furnace switch and call us immediately.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in San Diego

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that quits in the middle of the season. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: a new filter every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and two professional tune-ups a year, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

Take a look at the age of your system. Its age tells you a lot. Most well-maintained air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is past that range and is also showing rising bills, uneven temperatures, or repeated repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency replacement when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor ignored for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.

Common HVAC Problems in San Diego

Most local HVAC failures announce themselves before they happen. A breaker that trips every few days, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. Those are early signs of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're a quick visit. Left alone, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We find the root cause instead of treating symptoms, so small problems stay small.
